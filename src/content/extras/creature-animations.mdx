<ProjectDescription>
    I realised I hadn't tried to do any hand drawn animation for several years, so I decided to do a couple of "walk" cycles in an effort to revive those skills.

    These creatures are actually from an older version of the Snowbeat website which featured a slighly surreal farming theme mixing 3D renders and 2D drawings.

    These are animated at 24fps in Clip Studio Paint on a Surface Book 2, whereas previously I would have used Flash / Adobe Animate and a graphics tablet, so there was definitely a learning curve there.

</ProjectDescription>
